How to draw

  1. Pick a zone on the right (or add one).
  2. Click on the video to place corner points around the area just before the stop line where that road's vehicles queue.
  3. Press Finish shape (or double-click). Repeat for every road.
  4. Add crossing zones where pedestrians wait.
  5. Press Save.
